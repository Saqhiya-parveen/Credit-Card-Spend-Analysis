Executive Insights Summary

Critical Business Findings (DA-12)

  • ✓ Everyday Spends Anchor Loyalty: Grocery & Dining account for 42% of all swipes, driving retention, while Luxury & Travel generate 34% of revenue.
  • ⚠ The 180-Day Inactivity Cliff: 222 cardholders (18.5%) are inactive, locking up ₹4.8 Crores in idle credit lines with zero interchange revenue.
  • ⚡ Early Warning Window (45-90 Days): Intervention probability drops 68% after 90 days of inactivity. Automated SMS/App nudges must trigger at Day 45.
  • ★ Signature & Platinum Monetization: Top tiers command 62% of portfolio spend. Launching milestone spend waivers will unlock dormant high-tier cards.
